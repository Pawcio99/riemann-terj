8. term, territory and field of use;
9. treatment of sublicensing, affiliates and derivative commercial products;
10. termination provisions.

## Recommended accounting base

Where practical, the parties should prefer a clearly defined revenue base
rather than an undefined concept of "profit", because accounting treatment can
materially change reported profit.

A possible agreement may therefore use either:

- Gross Revenue attributable to the licensed product; or
- Net Revenue, with an exhaustive list of permitted deductions.

No percentage is imposed by this public document. The percentage must be
agreed in a separate signed commercial license.
